## What the PARTIALs led to

Seven of the nine PARTIALs are agent arithmetic or wording slips (tallies, a percentage, an over-broad claim) with correct tool output. Two pointed at tool output, and both were fixed afterwards:

- **s06**: Flowscan's staking-events route labels HYPE delegations `currency: "USDC"` (the /validators page shows HYPE). `flowscan_staking_events` now returns `amountHype` and drops the wrong label.
- **a30**: `flowscan_address_staking` history rows had only Unix-ms times and the agent converted one wrongly. Rows now carry `timeIso`.
